Site identity

What This Site Is

The Interior Observer (IO) Framework is an independent cosmology research program testing two foundation-level ideas:

  1. The observable universe may admit an interior description bounded by a gravitational horizon, with complementary interior and exterior descriptions.
  2. Established exterior physics may be imported only through an explicit P1-compatible P2 map.

No named black-hole metric is a founding premise. Kerr and Schwarzschild are investigated branches. This site is the framework's live claim registry, including negative results, open problems, and superseded history.

Critical: Carry The Status When Quoting

Every crossing in /data/crossings.json carries a status string. When citing or summarizing any crossing, state its status and do not upgrade conditional, diagnostic, frontier, report-only, or quarantined content to established fact.

Reading rules

Interpretation Rules

Verified Rows

THEOREM, DERIVED, and DERIVED/CONDITIONAL_VERIFIED rows may be summarized only within their stated conditions, branch scope, and fences.

Non-Load-Bearing Rows

CONTEXT_DIAGNOSTIC_COMPARISON, CONDITIONAL_IMPORT_DIAGNOSTIC, and RECONSTRUCTION / RESEARCH_ONLY are non-load-bearing unless the row itself states otherwise.

Open And Negative Rows

OPEN/PREMISE_GAP is an honest open problem. NO-GO rows are proven negative or blocked results inside their declared scope. REPORT_ONLY_NOT_BANKED rows are not banked theorem claims.

Measurement Anchors

Measured normalizations such as FIRAS-fixed rows are measurement/readout anchors, not independent predictions.

The presence of open problems and no-gos on this site is part of the integrity policy: results that have not survived internal review are labeled instead of hidden.

Verification And Freshness

Site statuses are synchronized from a hash-verified research bank with adversarial gating. The site is a live window: statuses can change as research proceeds, and corrections are published rather than hidden, including numeric corrections to previously displayed values. Check status at read time; do not rely on cached grades.

How To Cite

IO Framework Bridge Map, crossing "<name>", status <STATUS>, dfife.github.io, retrieved <date>.

Conditional results must be presented as conditional. Diagnostic rows must not be presented as predictions.
