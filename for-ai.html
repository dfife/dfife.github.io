Site identity

Program hierarchy

Interior Observer Cosmology Lab is the lab identity. Its overarching scientific project is the Bound-or-Unbound Evidence Program, which gathers auditable graded evidence about whether the universe is bound or unbound.

  1. Schwarzschild black-hole universe — bound branch.
  2. Kerr black-hole universe — bound branch.
  3. Infinite/unbounded universe — unbound branch.

The three branches have equal scientific standing. IO Foundational Generating Principle Research is the operational/governance container only, not a fourth branch. The IO Framework remains a continuing archive and research body for bound-work, including its Schwarzschild and Kerr results, papers, calculator, lithium material, reproducibility releases, and historical Scorecard.

Doctrine

Never collapse these four fields

Compatibility

Read all three per_branch_validity entries. True multi-branch compatibility is allowed. UNTESTED is named check debt and never means incompatible.

Evidential assessment

Read evidential_assessment separately: direction, qualitative strength, basis/data lineage, independence group, and selector status. Do not infer a lean from branch applicability.

GR–QM cross-check

qm_gr_map_triage is always running on every current result. A blocked seam is local; it never halts the cross-check. not_relevant and untested-with-debt are honest outcomes.

Selector posture

A universe selector is optional and non-primary. Do not promote a compatible result or an evidential lean into a selector. Recognize a selector only when a governed result explicitly establishes one.

Aggregation rule: do not add Schwarzschild and Kerr as two independent bound votes. Preserve independence_group. Report the overall program as Not yet assessed unless a governed aggregate record exists.

Critical: Carry The Status When Quoting

Every retained crossing in /data/crossings.json carries a historical status string. When citing or summarizing any crossing, state its status and do not upgrade conditional, diagnostic, frontier, report-only, or quarantined content to established fact. For current consumer-facing results, use /data/evidence-monitor.json.

Reading rules

Interpretation Rules

Verified Rows

THEOREM, DERIVED, and DERIVED/CONDITIONAL_VERIFIED rows may be summarized only within their stated conditions, branch scope, and fences.

Non-Load-Bearing Rows

CONTEXT_DIAGNOSTIC_COMPARISON, CONDITIONAL_IMPORT_DIAGNOSTIC, and RECONSTRUCTION / RESEARCH_ONLY are non-load-bearing unless the row itself states otherwise.

Open And Negative Rows

OPEN/PREMISE_GAP is an honest open problem. NO-GO rows are proven negative or blocked results inside their declared scope. REPORT_ONLY_NOT_BANKED rows are not banked theorem claims.

Measurement Anchors

Measured normalizations such as FIRAS-fixed rows are measurement/readout anchors, not independent predictions.

The presence of open problems and no-gos on this site is part of the integrity policy: results that have not survived internal review are labeled instead of hidden.

Verification And Freshness

The Evidence Monitor is a hash-bound projection of current public-safe ACTIVE_LOAD_BEARING MCP rows. Site statuses can change as research proceeds. Check status at read time, use the displayed source SHA, and do not treat the historical crossing inventory or Scorecard as the current aggregate.
