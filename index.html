Is the universe bound or unbound?

The Bound-or-Unbound Evidence Program gathers auditable, graded evidence across three equal scientific branches: a Schwarzschild black-hole universe, a Kerr black-hole universe, and an Infinite or unbounded universe. A result may apply to one, several, or all branches. Compatibility and evidential weight are tracked separately, and no selector is presumed or treated as the primary goal.

If the theory is correct, the math will just work.

Overarching scientific program

Three equal branches, one open question

“Equal” means equal standing in the evidence architecture. It does not mean that every result applies to every branch. Schwarzschild and Kerr are not two independent votes for boundedness and may not be double-counted.

Bound branch

Schwarzschild black-hole universe

The nonrotating bound branch. Historical Oppenheimer–Snyder and Schwarzschild results remain valid only inside their exact guards and are compared honestly with the other two branches.

Bound branch

Kerr black-hole universe

The rotating bound branch. Exact local Kerr constructions, curvature carriers, and their unresolved global or observer maps remain preserved without turning Kerr into a preferred foundation.

Unbound branch

Infinite or unbounded universe

The unbounded scientific branch, not merely a rhetorical foil. Local physics may be shared across branches; missing branch checks are recorded as debt rather than called incompatibility.

Governance: IO Foundational Generating Principle Research is the operational and governance container only—not a fourth scientific branch and not evidence that a unique generating principle exists. The realization-selector seam is optional and non-primary.

The CMB is not direct Hawking radiation.

Under the investigated universe-mass-to-Kerr dictionary, the Kerr Hawking temperature is at most 2.7264×10−31 K, while FIRAS measured 2.7255 K. This rules out that branch's direct Hawking-source reading; it does not select the foundation geometry.

IO Framework bound-work architecture

Three gates must connect without circularity

Inside the investigated Kerr branch, an exact Kerr-shaped region is not automatically the universe’s global event horizon, and a global horizon is not automatically an observed sky. The continuing IO Framework archive keeps all three proof obligations separate.

Local geometry

Established in a controlled domain: exact Doran–Kerr initial data, a one-sided stable marginal surface, and a local first-order spin carrier.

  • Exact open Kerr collar
  • Small symbolic spin
  • Local MOTS stability
  • Magnetic-Weyl response

Global causal placement

Open: the local collar has not been identified noncircularly with the complete cosmological event horizon within the governed development.

  • Global horizon membership
  • Generator completion
  • Global Kerr-horizon continuation
  • Interior/exterior relation

Observer readout

Open: no accepted map yet transports the intrinsic geometry of that same physical Kerr horizon to the observer’s full sky.

  • Same-horizon endpoint map
  • Caustic and degree control
  • Independent spin distortion
  • No numerical |χ|
