Independent cosmology · active Kerr branch · explicit claim boundaries

A closed Kerr-interior cosmology, tested without fitting the outcome.

Interior Observer treats the observable universe as a finite, closed Kerr black-hole interior. That is the working research premise, not an established observation. The governed bank now contains an exact small-symbolic-spin Kerr collar, a local one-sided marginally outer trapped surface (MOTS) stability result, and a nonzero local spin-sensitive curvature carrier. It does not yet prove global event-horizon identity, determine the spin magnitude, or supply an observer-to-horizon readout.

If the theory is correct, the math will just work.

What changed

Three findings that define the active branch

Kerr geometry is now constructed locally.

The active result is stronger than simply assuming rotation: an exact Doran–Kerr collar and local stability theorem are supplied. A separate context-only closed-S3 construction shows mathematical compatibility, but does not prove global event-horizon membership.

Ask IO about the exact Kerr collar →

The CMB is not direct Hawking radiation.

For the fixed universe mass, the Kerr Hawking temperature is at most 2.7264×10−31 K, while FIRAS measured 2.7255 K. The active typing is interior recombination radiation received through the Thomson line-of-sight channel.

Ask IO about the CMB source disposition →

Spin is carried, but not measured.

A local magnetic-Weyl curvature coefficient responds nontrivially to signed spin. Separately, one same-horizon intrinsic distortion would determine unsigned spin exactly. No governed observer map currently supplies that distortion, so no numerical |χ| is claimed.

Research architecture

Three gates must connect without circularity

An exact Kerr-shaped region is not automatically the universe’s global event horizon, and a global horizon is not automatically an observed sky. The active program keeps these proof obligations separate.

Local geometry

Established in a controlled domain: exact Doran–Kerr initial data, a one-sided stable marginal surface, and a local first-order spin carrier.

  • Exact open Kerr collar
  • Small symbolic spin
  • Local MOTS stability
  • Magnetic-Weyl response

Global causal placement

Open: the local collar has not been identified noncircularly with the complete cosmological event horizon within the governed development.

  • Global horizon membership
  • Generator completion
  • Global Kerr-horizon continuation
  • Interior/exterior relation

Observer readout

Open: no accepted map yet transports the intrinsic geometry of that same physical Kerr horizon to the observer’s full sky.

  • Same-horizon endpoint map
  • Caustic and degree control
  • Independent spin distortion
  • No numerical |χ|
