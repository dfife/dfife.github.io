Independent cosmology research

Is the universe bound or unbound?

We compare three possible large-scale pictures of the universe. Two are finite, black-hole-universe models; one is an infinite or otherwise unbounded model. We ask what each scientific result supports, challenges, or simply leaves open.

What the evidence says today

There is no clear answer yet.

The empirical evidence is mixed, with a fragile, very slight tilt away from one specific rotating bound model. If that single comparison is removed, the tilt disappears; it does not show that the Infinite model is correct. Separate mathematical bookkeeping makes the bound model family look somewhat simpler, but that is not observational evidence and the two judgments are not added together.

No result currently selects a winning model.

Six words that make the site easier to read

IO

“Interior Observer”: the viewpoint of an observer who may live inside a larger gravitational system.

Bound

A universe with a finite global enclosure or gravitationally bounded structure in the model being tested.

Unbound

A universe whose global spatial extent or development is not enclosed in that way.

Branch

One model tested on equal footing, with its assumptions stated rather than silently shared.

Compatibility

Whether a result can apply in a model. This alone is not evidence that the model is true.

Evidence lean

A modest direction suggested by evidence. It is not a final verdict or a model selector.

Three models · equal scientific standing

The branches being compared

The first two are different models on the same “bound” side. They are never counted as two independent votes merely because there are two of them.

Bound model

Schwarzschild

A nonrotating black-hole-universe model. Older Oppenheimer–Snyder and Schwarzschild calculations remain available under their original assumptions.

Bound model

Kerr

A rotating black-hole-universe model. Some local mathematics is established, while the global geometry and observer connection remain open.

Unbound model

Infinite or unbounded

A genuine comparison model, not a fallback. A result can apply here and in either bound model at the same time.

Important: the Foundational Generating Principle project organizes the work. It is not a fourth model and contributes no extra vote.
