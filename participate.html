Enthusiasts and skeptics welcome

Participate in the evidence program

The question is deliberately open: is the universe bound or unbound? Careful criticism, independent reproduction, and well-sourced counterevidence are as useful as supportive results. The program compares three equal scientific branches and does not presume a selector.

Use Discord for questions, reproductions, counterevidence, and collaboration. A discussion becomes a governed lab result only after review and promotion to the MCP Cosmology Lab.

For enthusiasts

Reproduce before extending

Choose a paper or calculator result, rerun its public bundle, and report exact versions, inputs, outputs, and discrepancies. If you see a new implication, identify the branch assumptions and keep compatibility separate from evidential weight.

  • Independent calculation or code reproduction
  • Primary observational data relevant to one or more branches
  • A typed GR–QM connection or obstruction
  • A branch comparison that does not tune to the desired outcome

For skeptics

Attack the strongest link

A precise no-go, counterexample, missing premise, source-contamination finding, or reproducibility failure advances the program. Name the exact record, equation, branch, and guard you are challenging so the objection can be checked.

  • Hidden fit, normalization, selector, or post-target choice
  • Counterexample inside the claim’s declared admissible class
  • Incorrect transfer between Schwarzschild, Kerr, and Infinite branches
  • Observation or standard result that the current bank misses
Useful first message: say what you tested, which public URL or record you used, what you expected, what happened, and whether your point concerns compatibility, evidence lean, the GR–QM cross-check, or selector status. Contact david@fife.cc.
