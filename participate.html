Enthusiasts and skeptics welcome

Participate in the evidence program

The question is open: is the universe bound or unbound? You can help by asking a sharp question, finding counterevidence, checking a calculation, or explaining a better model. Supportive and adverse results are equally useful when their limits are clear.

Use Discord for questions, reproductions, counterevidence, and collaboration. Discussion is provisional. It becomes a reviewed lab result only through a separate internal review; the public website then reports current findings where appropriate.

Newcomer route

Ask or challenge in plain language

Start with a claim that interests or bothers you. Say what you think it means, ask which assumption carries it, or bring a source that points another way. You do not need a record ID, equation number, or specialist vocabulary.

  • “What observation most challenges the Kerr model?”
  • “Why does compatibility not count as evidence?”
  • “This source appears to contradict the site—has it been considered?”
  • “Could you explain this result without the technical codes?”

Formal expert route

Reproduce or attack a specific result

Choose a public paper, release, calculator output, or Evidence Monitor entry. Report the exact source, assumptions, code and inputs you used, then identify the mathematical, observational, or dependency issue.

  • Independent calculation or code reproduction
  • Counterexample inside the stated assumptions
  • Hidden fit, normalization, or post-result choice
  • Incorrect transfer between the three universe models
Useful first message: say what you are questioning or testing, link any public source you used, and explain what changed your mind or failed to reproduce. Technical contributors can add exact versions, inputs, and outputs. Contact david@fife.cc or use the public Discord.
