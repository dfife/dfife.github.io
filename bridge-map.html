Where gravity meets quantum physics

General relativity (GR) describes gravity and large-scale spacetime. Quantum mechanics (QM) describes matter and fields at very small scales. A cosmological idea that uses both must say exactly where the two descriptions connect—and where that connection is missing.

The lab performs this check continuously across all three universe models. A local gap does not stop the whole program, and a successful connection is not evidence that a particular universe model is true.

What this board contains: 70 crossings preserved from the historical IO Framework papers. They are useful technical history, not 70 confirmations, not a bound-versus-unbound vote, and not a universe selector. For the GR–QM status of current results, use the Evidence Monitor.

Read a card in this order

  2. What was actually found?
  3. Which assumptions does it need?
  4. What remains open?

Why consistency matters

A formula cannot quietly switch between a gravitational geometry, a quantum state, a clock, or an observer. This check exposes those hand-offs rather than treating them as automatic.
